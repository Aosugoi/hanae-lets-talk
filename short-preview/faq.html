BEFORE YOUR FIRST HELLO

A few things you might be wondering.

Do I need to know any Japanese?

Beginner sessions start with simple, useful phrases. Tell Hanae where you’re starting during your introduction.

When are the children’s kanji sessions?

The planned time window is 3–6 p.m. Japan time (JST / UTC+9). This is an availability window, not a three-hour lesson. Days, session lengths, ages, format and parent involvement will be confirmed before booking.

Can I learn online or in person?

Online lessons and in-person practice in Fukuoka are part of the offer being developed. Locations and service-specific formats are to be agreed.

What teaching experience does Hanae have?

Hanae informally helps friends learn Japanese and is studying toward a Japanese teaching qualification. She is preparing her first paid teaching offer.

Can I book now?

This is a concept preview. Prices and start dates are not final. Introduction buttons demonstrate the flow without sending a message or booking.
