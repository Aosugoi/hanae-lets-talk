25 color themes for Hanae

The same homepage in 25 palettes from Sanzo Wada’s A Dictionary of Color Combinations, Vol. 1. Each example uses one color for the introduction box and sticker and the other for the trial button. The cream background is shared across the examples. Themes 01–05 are the original set.
