04 / YOUR PERSON IN JAPAN

Meet Hanae.
Come as you are.

こんにちは、ハナエです。

Outgoing, curious, and always happy to meet someone new.

Hanae grew up in and around Fukuoka. Travelling the world helped her develop her English and a love of connecting with people from different walks of life.

A mother of three and a keen reader, she enjoys helping friends who don’t speak Japanese. She is currently working toward a Japanese teaching qualification and developing her first paid teaching offer.

She knows that school communication can be difficult in another language. Her own children have found kanji challenging too—so she wants to help children practise with patience and encouragement.

And yes, she loves anime too. Ask her about Megumi, Rengoku or Vegeta.
