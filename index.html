03 / YOUR PERSON IN JAPAN

Meet Hanae.
Come as you are.

こんにちは、Hanaeです。

Outgoing, curious, and always happy to meet someone new.

Hanae grew up in and around Fukuoka. Travelling the world helped her develop her English and a love of connecting with people from different walks of life.

She’s a mother of three, a reader, and someone who enjoys a good conversation. She enjoys helping friends who don’t speak Japanese and is currently working toward a Japanese teaching qualification.

She also loves Jujutsu Kaisen, Dragon Ball and Demon Slayer. Megumi Fushiguro is her favourite in Jujutsu Kaisen; she also loves Rengoku and Vegeta. Tell her about your favourites.

呪術廻戦、ドラゴンボール、鬼滅の刃も大好き。呪術廻戦の推しは伏黒恵。煉獄杏寿郎とベジータも大好きです。あなたの推しも教えてください。

As a parent, Hanae understands that school communication can feel difficult in another language. She also wants to help school-age children practise kanji—something her own children have found challenging too.

３人の子どもを育てる母として、学校とのやりとりや子どもの漢字学習を身近に感じています。毎日の生活で使う日本語を、一緒に少しずつ練習しましょう。

BEFORE YOUR FIRST HELLO

A few things you might be wondering.

Do I need to know any Japanese?

The proposed beginner sessions start with simple, useful phrases. Tell Hanae where you’re starting during your introduction.

Can I practise online before my trip?

Yes, online travel preparation is part of this concept. Session lengths, prices and availability still need Hanae’s confirmation.

What would a café session look like?

The idea is to rehearse an order together, practise a short exchange, and try it yourself when you feel comfortable. Fukuoka locations and practical arrangements are still being developed.

Can my child practise kanji with Hanae?

Hanae would like to offer kanji support for school-age children. Talk with her about your child’s current learning and goals. Ages, parent involvement and session arrangements still need to be agreed before booking.

What teaching experience does Hanae have?

Hanae enjoys informally helping friends learn Japanese and is working toward a Japanese teaching qualification. She is developing her first paid teaching offer; this preview does not present her as an already qualified teacher.

When will lessons be available?

Hanae has suggested daytime availability around 9 or 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Japan time. Specific days, session times and a start date have not yet been confirmed.

Can I book a lesson now?

This is a concept preview. The introduction buttons let you explore the flow without sending a message or making a booking.
